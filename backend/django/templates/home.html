About CampusHub

CampusHub simplifies academic management by providing one platform for attendance, course management, announcements, student records, faculty activities, and administrative tasks. It improves communication and enhances productivity across the campus.

👨‍🎓 Student Portal

View attendance, enrolled classes, profile, notices, and academic information.

👨‍🏫 Faculty Portal

Manage attendance, assigned classes, subjects, announcements, and student records.

🛡 Admin Dashboard

Assign classes and faculty, manage students, departments, users, and system settings.

📊 Attendance Management

Faculty can mark attendance for their assigned classes while students can view attendance reports.

📢 Announcements

Share important notices with specific classes, departments, or the entire campus.

📚 Course Management

Manage departments, classes, subjects, semesters, and faculty assignments efficiently.
